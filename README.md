## 2. Design System & Brand Identity

- **Visual Theme:** Luxury modern barbershop aesthetic.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Canvas Deep Black: `#101010`
  - Structural Charcoal: `#141414`, `#161616`, `#191919`
  - Artisan Gold Accents: `#C8A45D`, `#DFBF77`, `#B0873E`
  - High-Legibility Type: `#FFFFFF` (Primary), `#B8B8B8` (Secondary)
- **Typography:**
  - Display & Headlines: `Cinzel` (Google Fonts Luxury Serif)
  - Body & Micro-copy: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Google Fonts Modern Geometric)
- **Adherence:**
  - Zero unboxed static metadata pills
  - Strict Top Bar Contract (Wordmark — 5 Single-line Links — Call to Book Action)
  - No fake AI scores or mechanical telemetry tickers
  - Mobile sticky bar constrained to under 15% viewport height

## 3. Website Architecture

1. **Navigation Bar**: Sticky luxury navigation with brand wordmark, smooth scrolling navigation links, direct phone action, and mobile hamburger drawer.
2. **Hero Section**: High-resolution luxury barber chair photography with measured scrim, "LOOK SHARP. FEEL CONFIDENT." headline, dual CTAs ("Book Your Appointment", "Explore Our Services"), and 4-column trust strip.
3. **About Section**: "MORE THAN JUST A HAIRCUT" featuring artisan shear craft photography and 4 core values (Quality Haircuts, Relaxed Atmosphere, Professional Service, Attention to Detail).
4. **Services Section**: 6 industry-standard placeholder services (Classic Haircut, Modern Haircut, Fade Haircut, Beard Trim, Combo, Kids Cut) with duration estimates, "Price upon request" badges, and interactive category filtering.
5. **Gallery Section**: Responsive photo grid with category tabs and interactive Lightbox modal with keyboard navigation.
6. **Customer Reviews Section**: 4.3 / 5 Google rating display, 122 review badge, verbatim verified review themes, and direct link to Google Maps.
7. **Call-to-Action Section**: "YOUR NEXT GREAT HAIRCUT STARTS HERE" with direct dial and directions launcher.
8. **Contact & Location**: Verified physical address, direct phone with copy buttons, Google Maps embed, and business hours table.
9. **Footer**: Brand summary, quick links, contact info, social placeholders ("[Coming Soon]"), and client handover trigger.
10. **Interactive Modals**:
    - **Call to Book Modal**: Direct phone dialing with checklist of haircut preferences.
    - **Client Presentation & Handover Guide**: Blueprint to present to Joel outlining what's verified vs. what he can personalize in minutes.
